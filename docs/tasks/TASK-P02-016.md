## Objective and observable result

Give every live record a DEC-083 replay key: the market records from `live_records.normalize()` and the derived `VenueStatus`, validity `GapInterval` and `MaintenanceWindow` records from `live_validity.derive_live_validity()`. Provide a sort helper that orders a mixed list by that key. DEC-083's record noted that P02's derived records "gain the key fields on a later card"; this is that card.

**Design.** The key is computed by `live_validity.replay_key(record)`, not stored as new fields. This leaves the existing records, their `record_id`s and #44's dataclasses unchanged:
- `(available_at, record_rank, source_id, instrument_id, source_event_time in ns, sequence, version, field 8)`.
- `record_rank`: 0 market, 1 `VenueStatus`, 2 `GapInterval`, 3 `MaintenanceWindow`.
- `VenueStatus` and `MaintenanceWindow` use the instrument `kraken:status`; a `VenueStatus` keeps its `recv_ns × 100 + index` sequence.
- A `GapInterval` or `MaintenanceWindow` takes its `available_at` in µs as its event time, and sequence 0. Field 8 is used only by `MaintenanceWindow`: `source`, `event_id` as a string, then `excluded_start` (or `announced_start` when withdrawn).
- `replay_order()` drops quarantined `Record`s, as the contract requires. `all_records()` returns a `LiveValidity`'s records in that order.
