This map covers P01 task 3. Each required observation gets a **class**: accessible, conditional, unavailable or unresolved. The registry's status vocabulary maps onto these classes as shown in its [method table](source-registry.md#method). "Accessible" means documented and free with no open eligibility or terms item; it does not mean access has been demonstrated.

Each observation also has a **category**:
- **observed:** the source reports it.
- **derived:** computed by the system from observed data.
- **inferred:** an estimate with stated uncertainty.
- **own-account:** generated by the system itself.
- **unavailable:** not obtainable at all.

The required observations come from the accepted baseline:
- the four observational layers and the contracts in v10 `27-ORDER-FLOW-AND-LEDGER-INTELLIGENCE.md` (lines 7–16 and 47–56);
- the `10-DATA.md` market schema and its v0.9 amendment;
- the domains of `25-MARKET-INTELLIGENCE-SOURCE-REGISTER.csv`;
- the market-calendar row of `24-MARKET-AWARENESS-AND-REGIME-DETECTION.md:58`.

## Consequences for the architecture

1. **CMP-01/02.** Design per venue. Every record carries its venue, feed identity and coverage label. Under DEC-028 there is no consolidated ETF source on £0.
2. **CMP-03.** It appears feasible for crypto venues, from live capture only and subject to F-4. Historical depth replay starts at the project's first capture.
3. **Daily-first baseline.** For crypto it appears feasible, subject to the SR-05 terms (F-4). For ETFs it is blocked until F-1 closes. If F-1 closes negatively, DEC-029 applies: the crypto daily baseline runs first and ETFs follow.
4. **REQ-001 (native events first where entitled).** It appears satisfiable for crypto live data, subject to F-4, and only at IEX-venue scope for ETFs (subject to F-1). The evidence for it belongs to G02–G04.
