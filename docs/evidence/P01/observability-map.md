# P01 observability map

[Task](../../tasks/TASK-P01-001.md) · [Source registry](source-registry.md) · [P01 phase](../../phases/01-research-and-data-access/README.md) · [Component register](../../traceability/components.md)

**Status: research evidence for G01, 2026-10-24, under the research assumptions in the [source registry](source-registry.md#research-assumptions).** This map covers P01 task 3: it classifies each required observation as **accessible**, **conditional**, **unavailable** or **unresolved**, and records the consequence. "Accessible" means documented and free with no unverified eligibility item; it does not mean demonstrated.

The required observations come from the accepted baseline:
- the four observational layers and the contracts `NativeMarketEvent`, `BookState`, `FlowFeature` and `OnChainEvent` in v10 `27-ORDER-FLOW-AND-LEDGER-INTELLIGENCE.md`;
- the market schema in `10-DATA.md`;
- the context domains of `25-MARKET-INTELLIGENCE-SOURCE-REGISTER.csv`.

Each observation also has a category: **observed** (the source reports it), **derived** (computed from observed data), **inferred** (an estimate with stated uncertainty), **own-account** (generated by the system) or **unavailable**.

## Consequences for the architecture

1. **CMP-01/02:** design per venue, with every record carrying venue, feed identity and coverage label. There is no consolidated ETF source on £0.
2. **CMP-03:** feasible for crypto venues with live capture only. Historical replay of depth starts at the project's first capture.
3. **Daily-first baseline:** feasible for crypto subject to the SR-05 terms. For ETFs it is blocked until a licence-clear daily source is found (F-1).
4. **REQ-001 (native events first where entitled):** is met for crypto live data. For ETFs it is met only at IEX-venue scope.
